# Reporting and Progress data policy

Kotaana reports are descriptive views of data actually recorded for the authenticated athlete.

## Rules

- Never invent a workout, GPS session, weight record, check-in, meal log, plan or progress photo.
- Never treat a missing health input as a normal/healthy value.
- Every report exposes a data status: complete, partial, or insufficient.
- Partial reports show which source categories are missing.
- Insufficient reports say that a complete report cannot yet be produced and show the recorded counts that do exist.
- Weight change is shown only when at least two weight-history records are available in the reporting period.
- Activity distance is summed from saved GPS activities.
- Workout completion is calculated from saved exercise-performance records, not generated plans alone.

## Current UI

The Progress section contains:

- 7-day recorded completion/activity metrics
- 30-day recorded metrics
- data-source inventory
- recorded weight trend
- progress-photo timeline
- explicit missing-data disclosure

The dashboard also shows a compact report-data status so users can see whether their reports are complete without entering Progress.
